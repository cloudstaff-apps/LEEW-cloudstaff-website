Employee-rights litigation · Los Angeles, California

Legal / Employment Law

About the client

A Southern California employment law firm focused exclusively on employee rights, representing workers across cases involving wage disputes, wrongful termination, workplace discrimination, workers' compensation, and personal injury. Operating on a contingency-fee basis, they have earned recognition for accessible, client-centered legal representation and have secured multi-million-dollar verdicts and settlements on behalf of the workers they serve.

The situation

Record employment law case filings across Los Angeles were generating unsustainable workloads: document review, client communications and case preparation consuming time attorneys needed for strategy and courtroom work.

Cloudstaff provided a nearshore team in Bogotá working US hours with bilingual English and Spanish capability, scaling from 2 specialists to 50+ professionals across two continents with zero attrition.

Before Cloudstaff

The challenge

California is the most litigation-heavy labor market in the United States, and in 2024 that pressure reached record levels. For a Los Angeles employment law firm built around fighting for workers' rights, that environment meant one thing: sustained, structural growth in demand. The challenge was keeping pace with it.

1

Capacity constraints

Record employment filings brought chains of support work beyond sustainable limits.

2

Talent barriers

Bilingual legal support roles were growing faster than conventional hiring could fill.

3

Operational bottlenecks

Procedural work pulled attorneys away from strategy and courtroom work.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

After evaluating options, the firm selected Cloudstaff and made a deliberate first move: two Customer Care Specialists in Bogota, Colombia, operating on US hours from 12:00 to 21:00. The scheduling decision ensured the support team was present and responsive throughout the entire US business day. Equally important was the bilingual English and Spanish capability those specialists brought to every client interaction from the very first call.

  • US-hours coverage. Bogotá team operating 12:00–21:00, present through the full US business day.
  • Cloudstaff Academy delivering dedicated training for US legal operations support.
  • Zero attrition underpinned by holistic engagement, celebrations and community programs.
  • Bilingual from day one. Native Spanish fluency serving the firm's Spanish-speaking client base.
  • Real-time visibility. MyStaff dashboard and CS Mobile app for firm leadership.
  • Near-continuous coverage. Philippine team onboarded in 2026 extends beyond US business hours.

Looking ahead

Built to last

This firm's experience offers a concrete example of what is possible when a US law firm builds a nearshore virtual staffing strategy with genuine intentionality. Starting with two Customer Care Specialists in late 2023 and growing to more than 50 dedicated professionals across two continents by 2026, while sustaining zero attrition throughout, reflects the kind of long-term thinking that turns a staffing decision into a structural advantage.

With teams continuing to grow across Colombia and the Philippines, the firm is still adding new roles, still deepening the model, and still building on a foundation that has not wavered since the first hire. Every new role added has been a deliberate choice, grounded in the confidence that the foundation beneath it is solid.
