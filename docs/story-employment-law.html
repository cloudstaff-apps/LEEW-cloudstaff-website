A Partnership Built with Intention

What began with two specialists answering client calls has grown into a fully integrated nearshore legal operation spanning two continents, sustained by a retention record that reflects genuine investment in the people behind every role.

Employee-rights litigation · Los Angeles, California

Legal / Employment Law

The situation

Record employment law case filings across Los Angeles were generating unsustainable workloads: document review, client communications and case preparation consuming time attorneys needed for strategy and courtroom work.

Cloudstaff provided a nearshore team in Bogotá working US hours with bilingual English and Spanish capability, scaling from 2 specialists to 50+ professionals across two continents with zero attrition.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

  • US-hours coverage. Bogotá team operating 12:00–21:00, present through the full US business day.
