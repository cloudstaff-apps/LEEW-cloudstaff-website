Before Cloudstaff

The challenge

As McConnell Dowell's reputation for tackling complex infrastructure projects expanded, so did the pressure on their operations. Their IT leadership faced three key challenges:

1

Overstretched teams

Internal IT staff overwhelmed by routine support tasks, crowding out strategic work.

2

Tough talent market

Recruiting and retaining skilled local IT professionals was a constant struggle.

3

Continuity risks

Without a trusted, integrated partner, operational stability was at risk on major builds.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

When McConnell Dowell decided to offload routine IT support, they needed a partner who could source the right people quickly, embed them into existing workflows without disruption, and scale the team as the relationship matured. Cloudstaff delivered on all three from the outset.

  • Workforce assessment identified the right skills and cultural fit up front.
  • Day-one readiness. Compliance, IT setup, and secure system access handled before starting.
  • Dedicated Growth Manager oversaw expansion from 1 to 8 team members without disruption.
  • AI-powered matching sourced candidates from a pool of 1M+ pre-screened professionals.
  • Hypercare onboarding. Dedicated team aligned systems, set KPIs, and resolved early issues.
  • Cloudstaff Academy provided ongoing, industry-specific training and upskilling.

Built to last

McConnell Dowell's partnership with Cloudstaff demonstrates that a virtual team can be far more than operational support.
