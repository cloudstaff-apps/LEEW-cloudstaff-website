About the client

A trusted name in trade services for over 60 years, this company earns loyalty through quality, reliability, and exceptional customer care, serving homeowners and businesses across Southeast Queensland.

The situation

As growth accelerated, this trusted trade services provider found that managing an offshore workforce from scratch (payroll, compliance, IT setup, staff coordination) was pulling focus away from the business itself.

Cloudstaff's fully managed, end-to-end solution removed the administrative burden entirely: the team grew from 5 to 9 members in just three months, driven by performance rather than a hiring target.

The challenge

As the business expanded, operational pressure behind the scenes began to build, making it harder to maintain momentum. Traditional hiring models were not keeping pace with the company's needs.

1

Back-end burden

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

In early 2024, this trusted trade services provider partnered with Cloudstaff to simplify and centralize offshore workforce management. What began as a referral from a trusted contact quickly became a turning point for the business.

  • End-to-end support covering recruitment, onboarding, payroll, compliance and device setup.
  • AI-powered recruitment and a pre-vetted talent pool delivered specialists with no ramp-up.
  • Dedicated account team with consistent check-ins and clear communication throughout.
  • Initial team of 5 deployed across customer service, dispatch and accounts payable.
  • Real-time access. StaffCentral and Customer View Portal streamline workforce oversight.
  • CS Academy & Rewards keeping the offshore team engaged and continuously developing.
