About the client

Founded in 1997 as a travel exchange business for timeshare and cruise membership owners, this travel membership platform built its reputation managing curated travel products and services through B2B and B2B2C channels for corporate partners and commercial membership organizations. Following a private equity acquisition in 2019, the company focused on operational efficiency and scalable growth across its travel and leisure portfolio.

The situation

Following a private equity acquisition in 2019, this travel membership platform needed to move faster and operate more efficiently, but fixed staffing costs were outpacing output and inconsistent service quality was putting key partner relationships at risk.

What began as staff augmentation evolved into a full-scale virtual sales operation: 225 representatives generating $50M+ in annual cruise sales at a 34% net profit margin.

The challenge

As growth pressures intensified following a private equity acquisition in 2019, this travel membership platform found its existing staffing model struggling to keep pace with where the business needed to go.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

In 2018, this travel membership platform engaged Cloudstaff to optimize non-sales functions under a staff augmentation model, embedding skilled Cloudstaffers directly alongside internal teams and Global In-house Centers. When confidence in the model was established, a cruise sales pilot followed in 2022.

  • Embedded workforce model. Cloudstaffers worked directly within existing workflows with in-house responsiveness.
  • Full-stack support. Talent sourcing, onboarding, Cloudstaff Academy training, compliance and tech setup.
  • Higher booking values. Average booking rose from $1,200 to $1,380 as sales capability deepened.
  • Scoped pilot, real stakes. Cruise sales pilot launched with 20 inbound and outbound agents.
  • Enterprise technology. Real-time dashboards gave US leaders visibility into activity and KPIs.
  • 40% labor cost savings reinvested into training, technology and team development.

The results

Looking ahead

Built to last

The Cloudstaff partnership has long since moved beyond its original scope. It is now a structural part of the company's operational model, and one of the more durable competitive advantages the business has built over the past several years.

For businesses in travel, membership services, and adjacent sectors navigating similar pressures on margins, talent supply, and technology adoption, this story offers something worth paying attention to. When virtual staffing is deployed with genuine intention, embedded within real workflows, supported by enterprise-grade people technology, and given the time to mature, the results go well beyond cost savings. The team becomes a growth engine in its own right.
