Why we train everyone

We're making AI your Cloudstaffer's superpower

We believe AI skills make a person more valuable, not less. So we do not leave it to chance. Training all 8,000 of our Cloudstaffers is one of the biggest challenges we have taken on, and our Cloudstaff Academy trainers roll it out level by level.

Skills vary across any team. Some people already use AI every day, while others need guidance on prompting, checking outputs, protecting sensitive information, and applying AI in their role. A structured program closes that gap and moves a team from ad hoc experimentation to secure, consistent, and scalable AI use.

You do not need to become an AI expert. But the people you hire should be.

Industry and job-role specializations

At Levels 4 and 5 the training goes down different industry verticals and job roles. For example, we have an advanced course designed specifically for accountants.

These are advanced modules your Cloudstaffers take on a voluntary basis. Because they are customized, we ask clients to pay for these courses.

  • Your industry. Training built around the vertical you work in.
  • A job role. Advanced courses for a specific function, such as accounting.
  • Your business. Modules shaped to the specific needs of the client.

One aim across every level. All training, whether complimentary or paid, is designed to enable your Cloudstaffers to be more efficient and effective at what they do.

AI Coffee Hour

Practical AI briefings for Cloudstaff clients

AI is moving quickly, and you have a business to run. AI Coffee Hour is a regular client briefing held every two weeks, led by Cloudstaff's Chief AI Officer and open to all Cloudstaff clients. Each session runs for 30 minutes to an hour.

It is not a long technical webinar. Sessions are accessible, commercial, and relevant: real-world AI opportunities, practical use cases, emerging risks, productivity ideas, and examples of how businesses are using AI to improve their operations.

AI Survival Guide

A practical, no-fluff resource for business leaders

The guide explains in simple business terms how organizations can adopt AI across their operations. It highlights quick wins that deliver immediate productivity improvements, explores the longer-term strategic opportunities, and outlines the key risks to be aware of.

It includes 25 real-world case studies showing how organizations have used AI to improve the productivity, capability, and effectiveness of their offshore teams and support operations.
