About C2 Accounting

C2 Accounting is a 100% woman-owned accounting and bookkeeping firm serving small businesses, startups, and nonprofits across a wide range of industries including construction, retail, food and beverage, and the beauty sector. Built on a foundation of education, customized financial systems, and detailed accuracy, they go beyond traditional bookkeeping to help business owners understand their numbers, stay compliant, and make confident decisions at every stage of growth.

The situation

C2 Accounting needed to grow capacity while keeping prices competitive for the small businesses and nonprofits they serve, as first-time outsourcers navigating an unfamiliar process.

Cloudstaff's hands-on support made the transition straightforward, scaling C2 to six bookkeepers rated 10 out of 10 who integrate so naturally that clients can't tell they're not in the same office.

The challenge

As demand for C2 Accounting's services grew, so did the pressure to scale quickly without compromising the affordability and quality that their small business clients depended on. Having never outsourced before, the path forward was uncertain. Three challenges shaped the decision to look for a different kind of staffing partner:

1

Capacity limitations

Growing client demand outpaced conventional hiring timelines.

2

Affordability commitment

Growth couldn't come at the cost of accessible pricing for small businesses.

3

New to outsourcing

C2 needed a partner to guide them through an unfamiliar process with patience and care.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

C2 Accounting was looking for a provider that would treat them as a genuine client, not just another account. Cloudstaff guided them through every step of onboarding, making the transition into outsourcing feel manageable, well-supported, and far less daunting than anticipated.

  • Guided onboarding. Cloudstaff walked C2 through every step, making outsourcing far less daunting.
  • Same time zone operation enabling seamless day-to-day collaboration.
  • Seamless integration. Cloudstaffers work so naturally clients can't tell the difference.
  • Handpicked bookkeepers across functions, including a dedicated bookkeeping manager.
  • Client-facing capability established early in the engagement.

Built to last

For a firm whose entire model is built around making quality accounting accessible to small businesses and nonprofits, every hiring decision carries real weight. C2 Accounting could not afford to get it wrong, and they needed a partner willing to take the time to make sure they did not.
