The situation

As TM Strata's client base grew rapidly, mounting administrative work was pulling their Melbourne team away from the on-site client service that defines their reputation.

Cloudstaff provided a back-end team that already understood their systems and industry, cutting the in-house administrative load by 20% with up to 70% cost savings, and freeing local staff to focus on clients.
