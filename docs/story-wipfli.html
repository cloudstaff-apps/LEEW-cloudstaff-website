About Wipfli

Wipfli is a leading accounting and consulting firm providing audit, tax, and business consulting services across industries like financial services, healthcare, and manufacturing, with a growing focus on technology consulting and digital transformation.

The situation

Facing skyrocketing demand for business services, Wipfli knew their US-based talent couldn't keep pace. They made a strategic decision: to grow globally by outsourcing, expanding their team beyond borders. This bold move positioned them for sustained success in a competitive market.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

After exploring options in the Philippines, Wipfli selected Cloudstaff based on shared values around people development, talent investment, and employee experience. Cloudstaff's approach to building a strong work environment for its staff resonated directly with Wipfli's own people-first culture.

  • Launched with 4 accountants handling data entry and bill processing
  • Cloudstaff Academy delivered specialized training aligned to US accounting standards and practices
  • Four on-site training events conducted in 2024 alone to keep the team current on regulatory requirements and technology tools
  • MyStaff dashboard and CS Mobile app gave Wipfli leaders real-time visibility into team performance and in-progress activities
  • Time zone difference leveraged as a workflow advantage, with the Philippine team continuing work after US business hours closed
  • CS Cares and community engagement initiatives strengthened team cohesion and a sense of broader purpose

The outcome

The results

What started as a solution to a staffing constraint transformed into a structural competitive advantage. Wipfli expanded its service offerings and geographic presence while maintaining quality, responsiveness, and the people-first culture that defines the firm. Cost savings were not treated as an end in themselves but reinvested directly into training, technology, and team development, compounding the long-term value of the remote workforce.
