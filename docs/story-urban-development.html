About the client

A leading real estate and land use organization based in Washington, D.C., with a global network of 45,000+ professionals. They advance sustainable development and innovation through thought leadership, collaboration, and the promotion of industry best practices.

The situation

Managing a growing network of freelancers across multiple countries had created mounting compliance risk, inconsistent retention, and limited visibility into performance across a global operation.

Partnering with Cloudstaff in 2024, they replaced that fragmented model with a fully compliant, purpose-built remote team in Clark and Manila, delivering the structure and consistency their mission required.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

In 2024, this Washington D.C.-based organization partnered with Cloudstaff to transition from a fragmented freelance model to a fully managed, high-performing remote team based in Clark and Manila, Philippines. Cloudstaff carefully matched top talent to their specific needs, ensuring every candidate was skilled, mission-aligned, and onboarded compliantly from day one.

  • Structured hiring via Cloudstaff's RNI process for skills, mindset and cultural alignment.
  • Dedicated success teams providing consistent guidance and performance coaching.
  • CS Academy equipping staff with role-specific, industry-tailored upskilling.
  • Compliant onboarding fully aligned with Philippine employment laws and standards.
  • Full visibility. StaffCentral, Workbench and CS Tap deliver real-time control.
  • AI-powered tools streamlining workflows and reducing manual effort across the team.

Built to last

As the client continues to expand its global presence and drive forward its mission, the partnership with Cloudstaff has become more than just a solution for remote staffing; it's now a cornerstone of their operational growth strategy.
