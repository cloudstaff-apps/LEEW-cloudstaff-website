How Cloudstaff thinks about AI

Three things have to be true

People who can do more

Cloudstaffers are trained and equipped to use AI in their daily work, so the dedicated team you already rely on becomes faster, sharper, and more productive.

Evidence today

AI training and certification program, internal AI tooling, and a growing set of use cases.

AI with a defined job

Supervised Coworkers do specific work within defined responsibilities, rules, and review. AI needs a job description, and a human it reports to.

Evidence today

Live Coworker units, early client engagements, and in-production client workflows.

Built for business control

The same workforce infrastructure, security capabilities, and developing governance model that protects your human teams extends to AI work, one standard for both.

Evidence today

Existing security infrastructure and client-facing security work.

AI-enabled staffing

Human + AI solutions

Proactive, not prompted

A workforce member, not a widget

An AI Coworker isn't an assistant that sits and waits. It monitors activity, spots what needs attention, starts the task, and follows up, the way a reliable team member would. It has its own name, its own communication channels, and a place in your workforce and reporting systems, so the work is always visible and accountable.
