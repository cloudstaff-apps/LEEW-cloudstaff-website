About Leisurecom eCruising

Leisurecom Group is an Australian travel and lifestyle company specialising in end-to-end holiday, travel, and entertainment solutions through a portfolio of consumer-facing brands including Hightide Holidays, eCruising, and Entertainment. Operating from Queensland's Gold Coast, they serve both B2C travellers and travel industry partners, delivering tailored booking experiences and marketing campaigns that connect Australians with memorable moments at home and around the world.

The situation

Leisurecom eCruising needed to increase productivity and cut administrative load for their sales team, whose focus was being pulled away from revenue-generating work.

With support functions fully handled by Cloudstaff, the sales team refocused entirely on selling, generating significant additional revenue, with Cloudstaff agents in some cases outperforming their Australian counterparts.
