About Alliance Consumer Solutions

Alliance Consumer Solutions is a leader in medical collections, known for professionalism, responsiveness, and strong recovery results. The agency supports clients with dependable after-hours coverage and a commitment to service excellence.

The situation

Struggling with local hiring and retention worsened by COVID-19, Alliance needed consistent staffing and uninterrupted client call coverage in a high-stakes, client-facing industry.

Cloudstaff gave them the rare ability to interview and handpick every team member, delivering work ethic and extended-hours availability that consistently outperformed local expectations, with zero downtime through pandemic-era disruptions.

Before Cloudstaff

The challenge

When Alliance Consumer Solutions, a Mississippi-based medical collections agency, faced the staffing disruptions brought on by the pandemic, they encountered critical business challenges:

1

Hiring & retention

Traditional recruitment methods struggled to attract and retain talent through the pandemic.

2

Service disruptions

Increased turnover led to missed client calls, impacting recovery rates and client trust.

3

Recruitment opacity

Limited involvement in hiring made it hard to ensure candidates fit culture and standards.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

After a previous outsourcing experience that offered little transparency and inconsistent results, Alliance Collection Service needed a partner that would give them genuine control over who joined their team. Cloudstaff delivered that from the very first conversation.

  • Handpicked hiring. Alliance interviewed and selected every team member directly before any offer was made.
  • Badge Pack training ensured every hire was role-ready and fully prepared from day one.
  • 24/7 Command Center and backup support sites maintained uninterrupted operations.
  • 1M+ pre-screened pool. AI-powered matching sourced the right candidates fast.
  • Secure infrastructure. Company-managed encrypted devices deployed across the team.
  • Flexible scheduling kept collectors online and taking calls after the local office closed.
