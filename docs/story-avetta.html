About Avetta

Avetta is a global leader in supply chain risk management, helping companies connect with prequalified suppliers and ensure compliance, safety, and sustainability. Its cloud-based platform delivers real-time visibility into supplier performance and risks, enabling smarter decisions and sustainable growth.

The situation

As Avetta's global customer base grew, so did the demand for timely, high-quality support across multiple regions. They needed a partner who could scale quickly and maintain service excellence, without sacrificing the visibility, oversight, or customer-first values that defined their business.

What began as a Philippines-based support team has grown to 125+ Cloudstaffers spanning four regions, with dedicated Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking pipelines serving Latin American markets from Colombia.

Before Cloudstaff

The challenge

As Avetta expanded global customer support across North America, EMEA, APAC, and Latin America, the company faced several critical challenges:

1

Rigid models

Traditional BPOs offered little visibility or involvement in hiring, training, or daily management.

2

Culture & language gaps

Expanding into Latin America required fluent Spanish and Portuguese speakers with localized expertise.

3

Customer experience risk

Rapid scaling posed direct risks to maintaining world-class NPS and CSAT scores.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

Avetta partnered with Cloudstaff to overcome traditional outsourcing limitations by creating a collaborative, customer-first global support model. Cloudstaff structured the entire engagement around that principle, keeping Avetta closely involved at every stage from recruitment through to daily operations.

  • Customer View Portal. Active involvement in candidate review, interviews, and onboarding decisions at every stage.
  • Multilingual pipelines. Dedicated Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking professionals serve Latin American markets.
  • Real-time visibility. MyStaff and Workbench platforms track performance across all time zones.
  • 1M+ talent pool. Philippine-based candidates accessed for APAC and North American customer support.
  • Cloudstaff Academy training aligned every team member to Avetta's customer-first standards.
  • Cultural alignment. Employee engagement initiatives ensure team cohesion across regions.

The outcome

The results

Avetta successfully scaled global customer support without sacrificing quality, visibility, or the customer-first values that define the business. The partnership gave them the agility to enter new markets while keeping the standards that matter most firmly in place.

Looking ahead

Built to last

What began as a staffing solution has evolved into a strategic alliance, with Cloudstaff operating as a genuine extension of Avetta's team rather than a separate provider. Today, with more than 125 Cloudstaffers embedded across the Philippines and Colombia, the partnership is a cornerstone of Avetta's global growth strategy.

Parke has actively recommended Cloudstaff to other organisations navigating similar scaling challenges, pointing to the ability to grow customer support capability in a cost-effective way without losing oversight or control of the work being done.

As Avetta continues to scale across new markets, Cloudstaff has become more than a partner. It's now a cornerstone of their growth strategy.

For organizations facing the same scaling challenges, Avetta's journey proves what's possible when you choose the right partner: filling roles by virtual staffing builds a stronger, customer-focused foundation for long-term growth.
