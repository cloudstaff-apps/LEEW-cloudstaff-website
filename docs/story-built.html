About Built

Built is a 100% Australian-owned, digital-first construction contractor and one of Australia's largest private construction groups, delivering large-scale new builds, complex refurbishments, and intricate fitouts across commercial, government, health, education, and defence sectors. With over 1,300 employees, a secured pipeline of $7.5 billion, and operations spanning Australia, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom, Built has grown steadily since 1998 by combining the scale of a tier-one contractor with a genuine commitment to innovation and community outcomes.

The situation

Growing rapidly across Australia, Built needed a reliable way to handle increased project demands without stretching internal resources, in a construction market facing a persistent talent shortage.

Skilled Cloudstaff professionals integrated seamlessly into their workflows: 40+ staff across accounting, IT, digital transformation and quantity surveying, with virtually no turnover in a partnership that has now passed its tenth year.

Before Cloudstaff

The challenge

As one of Australia's largest private construction groups, Built had earned a strong reputation for delivering large-scale projects across the commercial, government, health, education, and defense sectors. But as the pace of growth accelerated, the pressures behind the scenes began to mount. Three interconnected challenges were making it increasingly difficult to scale without compromising on quality or focus:

1

Capacity constraints

Internal teams needed skilled supplementation to keep pace with project demands.

2

Talent shortage

Persistent scarcity of qualified people in Australian construction made conventional hiring difficult.

3

Focus dilution

Routine tasks consumed time needed for critical thinking and high-value project delivery.

What Cloudstaff delivered

The solution

Built chose Cloudstaff after recognizing a clear alignment in values around community, ethics, and people development. What began as a decision to supplement existing staff quickly became one of the most enduring partnerships in the business.

  • Shared values. Cloudstaff selected for alignment on community, ethics, and people development.
  • Collaborative onboarding let team members absorb processes and grow into their roles.
  • Competitive edge gained through freed-up capacity for critical thinking.
  • 40+ embedded staff across accounting, IT, digital transformation, and quantity surveying.
  • Near-zero turnover reflecting genuine values alignment from the outset.
  • CS Academy and a people-first culture keep team members engaged and committed.

Looking ahead

Built to last

What began as a practical response to rapid growth has evolved into one of Built's most enduring operational strengths. With 40 plus Cloudstaffers embedded across accounting, IT, digital transformation, and quantity surveying, the partnership has moved well beyond supplementing headcount into genuinely shaping how Built works and competes.

In a sector facing persistent talent shortages, Built's ability to keep growing without compromising quality or critical thinking time reflects exactly what a well-matched remote staffing partnership can deliver.

The near-zero turnover across the partnership is not incidental but rather the result of a values alignment that was deliberate from the start, and one that continues to hold.

For Australian construction businesses navigating the same pressures, Built's experience makes a compelling case: the right outsourcing partner does not just fill gaps. Over time, it becomes part of how you build.
