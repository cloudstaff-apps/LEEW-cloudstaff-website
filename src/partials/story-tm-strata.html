About TM Strata

TM Strata is a leading Melbourne-based owners' corporation strata management firm, known for reliable service, responsive client support, and expertise in managing residential and commercial strata properties.

As TM Strata's client base grew rapidly, mounting administrative work was pulling their Melbourne team away from the on-site client service that defines their reputation.

The solution

After evaluating multiple providers, TM Strata selected Cloudstaff based on their demonstrated understanding of the strata management sector and the systems the Melbourne team used every day. That meant immediate, meaningful contribution without a lengthy onboarding curve.

  • Full back-office ownership. 2 full-time virtual staff took on core administrative functions.

  • MyStaff portal and collaboration tools enabled seamless remote integration.
  • Complete coverage of notices, agendas, minutes, levy queries, and lot owner communications.
  • Cloudstaff Academy training aligned to strata industry standards and compliance.
  • Engagement programs ensured high responsiveness and consistent delivery throughout.
