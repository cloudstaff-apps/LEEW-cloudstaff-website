What Cloudstaff Q does

The control layer for a connected AI workforce

Cloudstaff Q is the infrastructure behind every AI Coworker. It provides a secure place to build and train them, an integration layer that connects to the APIs, platforms, and tools you already use, and the dashboards and logs that make every action reviewable for management and compliance. AI Coworkers do the work; Cloudstaff Q keeps it connected, governed, and accountable.

Your IP and data stay yours

Your know-how lives in your own repository on Cloudstaff Q, where your Coworkers can use it. Your knowledge, data, and intellectual property remain with your business, not the model.

Flexible hosting & data sovereignty

Run on Cloudstaff's private cloud, your own private cloud, or on-premise servers. You choose where your AI runs and where your data lives.

Multi-model access

Access to hundreds of AI models, so each job gets the right one and your business is not locked into a single vendor.

Integration layer

Dashboards & logs

Every communication and every decision is logged, with safety reporting built in, so work can be reviewed for quality and compliance.
